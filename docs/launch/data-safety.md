# Play Console: Data safety answers

Based on what the code actually does (checked in Phase 8). Re-check if a
new SDK or network call is added. Everything the person types (documents,
clients, items, amounts, photos, signatures) stays on the phone unless they
share it themselves or back it up to their own Google Drive.

## Network traffic in the app

| Destination | What is sent | When | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Drive API (`drive.appdata`) | Backup zip of the person's own data | Only after they connect Drive; manual or weekly automatic | `src/backup/service.ts`, `DriveModule.kt` |
| Google Play Billing | Purchase of `pro_lifetime` | When they buy or restore | `react-native-purchases` |
| RevenueCat | Purchase receipt/token, an anonymous app-generated user ID, device/OS/app version, country | When the store is queried, bought or restored | `src/billing/store.ts` |
| Sentry | Crash stack trace, device model, OS and app version, anonymous installation ID | After a crash, if a DSN is set and crash reports are on | `src/telemetry.ts` |

Sharing a PDF through WhatsApp, email, SMS or the share sheet is initiated by
the person and handled by the app they pick. Per Play guidance that is not
"collection" or "sharing" by this app.

## Answers

**Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes.

**Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS only).

**Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** Yes.
Everything is stored on the device: uninstalling deletes it. Drive backups are
deleted in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → [app] → Delete hidden app data.
Crash reports and purchase records can be deleted on request via the support email.

### Data types

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Optional? | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Financial info → Purchase history** | Yes | No | No (only if they buy) | App functionality (unlock Pro) | RevenueCat processes this as our service provider; service providers do not count as "shared". |
| **App info and performance → Crash logs** | Yes | No | **Yes** (Settings → Send crash reports) | Analytics (stability) | Sentry, as service provider. |
| **App info and performance → Diagnostics** | Yes | No | **Yes** | Analytics (stability) | Device model, OS version, memory at crash time. |
| **Device or other IDs** | Yes | No | No | App functionality (Pro), Analytics | Anonymous IDs generated by RevenueCat and Sentry. Not the advertising ID, not IMEI. |
| **Files and docs** | Yes* | No | **Yes** (only if they connect Drive) | App functionality (backup) | *Judgment call: the backup goes to the person's **own** Drive, not to us. Declaring it is the conservative choice. |

Not collected: name, email, phone, address, contacts, location, photos
(photos stay on the device and inside the person's own backup), messages,
web history, health, advertising ID. No ads, no analytics SDK beyond crash
reports.

**Contacts:** the "Import from contacts" button uses the Android contact
picker; the app reads only the one contact the person picks and never
uploads it. No `READ_CONTACTS` permission. Not "collected".

## Other declarations

- **Ads:** No ads.
- **App access:** All functionality is available without an account. No login.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over (business tool).
- **Content rating questionnaire:** Utility/productivity, no user-generated
  content shared with others, no user-to-user communication, no location
  sharing, digital purchases: yes.
- **Financial features declaration:** the app does not provide financial
  services (no payments processed, no lending, no banking). It creates
  invoices. If asked, choose "My app doesn't provide any financial features".
- **Permissions:** `INTERNET` (Drive, billing, crash reports),
  `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (asked only when reminders are turned on),
  `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` (re-schedule reminders after a restart),
  Play Billing. Camera is used through the system camera app (no
  `CAMERA` permission).
