**{APP_NAME}** · Last updated: {DATE}

{APP_NAME} ("the app") is made by {DEVELOPER_NAME} ("we"). This policy
explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

- There is no account and no sign-up.
- Your invoices, estimates, clients, items, prices, expenses, trips, photos
  and signatures are stored **only on your phone**. We never receive them.
- If you choose to back up to Google Drive, the backup goes to **your own**
  Google Drive, in a private folder only this app can read. We cannot see it.
- If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We receive a record that
  a purchase was made, not your card details.
- If the app crashes, it can send an anonymous crash report. You can turn
  this off in Settings.
- No ads. No tracking. We do not sell data.

## Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app is stored in the app's private storage on
your phone. It leaves your phone only when you choose to:

- **Send or share a document** (for example by WhatsApp, email or SMS). The
  document is handed to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy
  applies.
- **Save a backup file** to a place you choose.
- **Back up to Google Drive** (see below).

Android's own device backup may also include the app's data in your
personal Google account backup, as for any app.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to use a
hidden folder that belongs to this app in your Drive
(`drive.appdata`). Backups are stored there. The app cannot see any of your
other Drive files. You can disconnect at any time, and you can delete the
backups in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment
under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). To
confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives the
purchase receipt, an anonymous app-generated identifier, your country and
basic device and app version information. RevenueCat's privacy policy:
<https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

## Crash reports (optional)

When the app crashes, it can send a report to Sentry so we can fix the
problem. A report contains the technical error, your device model, Android
version, app version and an anonymous installation identifier. It does not
contain your documents, clients, amounts or anything you typed. You can turn
crash reports off in **Settings → Privacy and security → Send crash reports**.
Sentry's privacy policy: <https://sentry.io/privacy/>.

## Contacts, camera and photos

When you import a client from your contacts, Android shows its contact
picker and the app reads only the contact you pick. When you add a photo or
receipt, Android's photo picker or camera app is used and only the image you
choose is copied into the app. None of this is uploaded.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone. No
notification content is sent anywhere.

## Children

The app is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at
children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## Your rights and deleting your data

Because your data is on your phone, you control it: edit or delete it in the
app, or uninstall the app to remove it all. To delete Drive backups, see
above. To ask about or delete crash reports or purchase records linked to
your installation, email us at {SUPPORT_EMAIL}. If you are in the EU/UK you
have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection under the GDPR;
we will answer within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important
changes, tell you in the app.
